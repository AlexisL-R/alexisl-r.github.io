Call for contribution

If you wish to contribute a poem inspired by quiver mutation, feel free to send me an email at zine(a)tuta.com with object "Math poetry contribution" until 2026/12/31. Give your poem, the quivers yo uconsider, add some context to help me and I will collect work and send you a pdf version and a printable pdf. All languages are welcomed, but for the following languages: French, English, Dutch or German, I can comment and appreciate your work!
